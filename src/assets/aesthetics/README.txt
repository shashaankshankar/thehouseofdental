FACIAL AESTHETICS PHOTOS
========================
Drop real photos here with these EXACT filenames and they replace the
placeholders automatically:

  deka-laser.jpg        -- the DEKA laser unit in your treatment room
  emage-scanner.jpg     -- the Emage skin analysis scanner
  hydroderm-facial.jpg  -- a HydroDerm facial in progress

Specs: landscape 4:3, at least 1600x1200px, .jpg

WHERE TO GET THEM
-----------------
1. BEST: photograph your actual equipment once it's installed. Real photos of
   your own room beat any stock image and build far more trust.

2. ALSO GOOD: request official product photography directly from the
   manufacturers. Both companies provide high-resolution marketing images free
   to practices that own their equipment -- just email your rep and ask for
   their media/marketing asset kit.
     - DEKA: dekalaser.com
     - Emage Medical: emagemedical.com

DO NOT right-click and save images from their websites. Those are copyrighted
and using them without permission creates legal exposure for the practice.
Asking the rep takes one email and you get better files.

Until real photos are added, tasteful stock images display instead.

  microneedling.jpg     -- microneedling treatment in progress
