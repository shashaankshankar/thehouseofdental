Accessibility

The House of Dental is working to make this website usable for people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies.

The draft site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, scalable layouts, labeled controls, and direct phone access.

Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier or need information in another format, call (407) 678-1400. Please identify the page and the difficulty you experienced so it can be reviewed.
