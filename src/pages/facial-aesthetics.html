Laser Resurfacing

DEKA CO2 Facial Resurfacing Laser

The House of Dental offers DEKA CO2 facial resurfacing in Winter Park. A consultation considers the skin changes you want to address, your health history, and the recovery involved before recommending treatment.

Schedule a Consultation

CO2 resurfacing may improve selected texture changes, lines, or scars. Skin tone, previous treatments, and healing history affect suitability. The consultation should also address alternatives and who will perform your procedure.

Laser energy treats selected areas of the skin. Settings and the extent of treatment influence both the result and healing time. Discuss preparation, comfort measures, and the expected number of visits with your treating provider.

Explore CO2 Laser Resurfacing

What It Addresses

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Sun damage and pigmentation
  • Acne scarring and surgical scars
  • Uneven skin texture and tone
  • Enlarged pores
  • Overall skin laxity and dullness

What to Expect

Recovery and limitations

Redness, swelling, and a period of skin healing are expected considerations. Pigment changes, infection, and scarring are possible risks. Obtain a personal aftercare plan and recovery estimate before scheduling; there is no universal downtime or guaranteed result.

Microneedling

Microneedling creates small punctures in the skin as part of a controlled treatment. At The House of Dental, a skin consultation helps establish whether it is an appropriate option for your concerns.

Schedule a Consultation

Discuss the texture or scarring you want to improve and your previous skin treatments. Results vary, and more than one visit may be recommended. Ask which device will be used and about the experience of the treating provider.

Bring your medication and skin-care history. The team should explain preparation, comfort measures, and the planned treatment area. Do not assume that a product is safe to apply immediately after treatment simply because it is part of your normal routine.

Explore Microneedling

What It Addresses

  • Acne scarring and textural irregularity
  • Fine lines and early wrinkles
  • Enlarged pores
  • Skin laxity and loss of firmness
  • Uneven tone and dullness
  • Stretch marks and surgical scars

What to Expect

Aftercare and risks

Temporary redness, irritation, or bleeding may occur; infection and pigment changes are also possible. Suitability requires an individual assessment, including for darker skin tones. Follow your provider’s product and sun-protection instructions rather than adding treatments yourself.

Diagnostic Imaging

Emage 3D Skin Analysis

Emage 3D skin analysis provides images for a conversation about your skin at The House of Dental. It supports aesthetic planning and comparison over time rather than replacing a clinical examination.

Schedule a Consultation

Tell the team which changes you have noticed and what you want to understand. Your skin-care routine, past procedures, and sensitivities add context that images alone cannot provide.

The team reviews the captured images with you and explains how they inform treatment options. Ask which findings are relevant to your goals and what the imaging system can and cannot establish. A score or image is not itself a diagnosis.

Explore Emage 3D Skin Analysis

What It Addresses

  • Subsurface UV and sun damage
  • Pigmentation and melanin distribution
  • Vascular and redness patterns
  • Pore size and congestion
  • Wrinkle depth and density
  • Skin texture and surface irregularity

What to Expect

Planning and follow-up

The scan may support a discussion of HydroDerm facials, microneedling, or CO2 resurfacing. It does not mean that any particular treatment is required. Repeat imaging can support comparison, but changes should be interpreted alongside your clinical response.

Signature Treatment

Custom HydroDerm Facials

Custom HydroDerm facials at The House of Dental combine cleansing, exfoliation, and hydration. The consultation focuses on your skin concerns, sensitivities, and the products already in your routine.

Schedule a Consultation

This service focuses on surface cleansing and hydration. Discuss dullness, dryness, or congestion with the team so that the steps and products can be selected appropriately. A facial is not a substitute for assessment of a persistent rash or changing skin lesion.

Tell the team about allergies, sensitive skin, recent procedures, and any active irritation. Product selection and exfoliation intensity should reflect that information. Ask how the proposed facial differs from the other options available.

Explore HydroDerm Facials

What It Addresses

  • Dullness and dehydration
  • Congested and enlarged pores
  • Blackheads and surface buildup
  • Uneven tone and early pigmentation
  • Fine lines from moisture loss
  • Pre-event skin preparation

What to Expect

After your facial

Skin response varies, and flushing or irritation can occur. Follow the team’s instructions about home products and sun protection. Schedule a first treatment with enough flexibility to see how your skin responds before an important event.
