Meet the Doctor

Dr. Mainak Patel, DMD

Dr. Mainak Patel grew up in Central Florida and graduated from Winter Park High School. He has enjoyed creative engineering and sciences from an early age, participating in numerous science fairs and volunteering for Habitat for Humanity. His passion for math, science, and engineering brought him to healthcare.

He earned a Bachelor of Arts and Sciences degree at the University of Florida, majoring in Microbiology and Cell Science, and continued at the University of Florida College of Dentistry, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD).

Dr. Patel’s care philosophy is to understand each patient’s wants and needs before discussing a plan. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his wife and family, traveling, and playing sports.
