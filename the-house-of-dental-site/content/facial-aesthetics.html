People, process & safety

Required before a public page

Who performs these treatments?

No performing provider or publishable qualification is confirmed. Add only a named provider and verified training after practice and clinical approval.

How should a consultation be explained?

  1. Confirm the active offering and provider.
  2. Discuss the patient's goals, history, candidacy, and alternatives.
  3. Explain the approved safety, recovery, limitations, and care language.
  4. Enable the consultation CTA only after the destination and disclosures are approved.

What should not be promised?

Do not say a treatment is safe for all skin types, has essentially no downtime, guarantees an outcome, or produces the same result for every patient. Recovery and suitability must remain individualized.
